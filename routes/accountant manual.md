1. Purpose
This manual provides official procedures for accountants to:

manage fee structures (vote heads),
record student fee payments,
allocate payments by vote-head priority,
manage finance vouchers and transactions,
print compliant accounting documents,
reconcile daily accounts.
2. Scope
Applies to all accountant users handling:

Student Finance
Fee Structures
Finance Transactions
Payment Vouchers
Receipt and Statement Printing
3. User Access
Only authorized users with Accountant role may perform these tasks.
Principal retains approval and oversight powers.

4. Core Modules and Their Purpose
4.1 Student Finance
Student fee accounts
Payment recording
Vote-head tracking
Receipt and statement printing
4.2 Fee Structures
Annual fee setup by category (Day Scholar/Boarder)
Vote-head definitions
Parent vs capitation splits
Term-based amounts (T1, T2, T3)
4.3 Finance
Income and expense recording
Payment voucher creation, search, and printing
5. Standard Procedures
5.1 Create / Update Fee Structures
Open Fee Structures.
Create annual structure (e.g., 2026 Day Scholars).
Add vote-head lines:
Govt capitation
Parent amount
Term 1, Term 2, Term 3
Save and verify totals.
Control: Do not modify active structures without approval.

5.2 Set Up Student Fee Account
Go to Student Finance.
Select student.
Assign fee structure.
Confirm total due and sponsor details (if any).
Save.
This generates/links vote-head expectations for that student.

5.3 Record Student Payment (Normal)
In Record Student Payment, fill:
Student
Amount
Method
Date
Receipt no (optional if auto)
Save.
Print receipt.
5.4 Record Student Payment (Priority Allocation)
Use when parent directs distribution (example: Lunch 1500, RMI 500).

Enable Use vote-head priority allocation.
Add vote-head rows and amounts.
Confirm:
Split Total = Payment Amount
Difference = 0.00
Save and print receipt.
5.5 Verify Posting
After saving:

Confirm receipt appears under Payment Receipts.
Confirm paid/balance updates under Student Fee Accounts.
Click View Vote Heads and verify posted amounts.
5.6 Finance Transactions
Open Finance.
Record all income and expenses.
Add method and reference.
Save and verify.
5.7 Payment Voucher Processing
Create voucher in Finance.
Verify auto voucher number.
Fill payee, amount, purpose, method.
Print voucher.
Ensure signatures:
Principal approval
Accountant signature
Payee signature
6. End-of-Day Reconciliation
Match receipt totals to cash/bank records.
Match finance entries to supporting docs.
Confirm all receipts and vouchers printed.
File documents by date and number.
Report variances immediately.
7. Error Handling
Vote-head table empty: check student fee structure assignment and refresh.
Allocation mismatch: make split total equal payment.
Missing receipt in list: refresh and clear filters.
Print issues: re-open print route and verify saved record.
8. Internal Controls
Do not share login credentials.
Do not delete transactions without written authorization.
Use references for every entry.
Keep signed printed receipts/vouchers.
Follow chronological filing and daily balancing.

2) Trainer Script Format (Onboarding Practical Session)
SESSION TITLE
Accountant System Onboarding – Practical Training Script

DURATION
2.5 to 3 hours

AUDIENCE
New accountants / finance clerks

TRAINING OBJECTIVE
By end of session, trainee must independently:

create fee structure,
assign structure to student,
record payment with/without priority allocation,
verify vote-head breakdown,
print receipt/statement/voucher,
perform daily close checks.
A. Trainer Preparation (Before Session)
Confirm trainee login works.
Confirm test student records exist.
Confirm vote heads and fee structures are loaded.
Confirm printer or PDF print option available.
Prepare 3 scenarios:
normal payment,
split priority payment,
voucher payment.
B. Session Flow
Step 1 – Orientation (10 min)
Trainer says:

“Today you will learn Student Finance, Vote-Head Allocation, and Voucher workflows.
Every payment must be verifiable in receipts and vote-head breakdown.”

Show:

Student Finance menu
Finance menu
Fee Structures menu
Step 2 – Fee Structure Demo (20 min)
Trainer action:

Open Fee Structures.
Show structure fields (year/category/vote heads).
Show term split and totals.
Trainee task:

Create one sample structure with 2–3 vote heads.
Checkpoint:

Structure saves successfully.
It appears in list.
Step 3 – Student Fee Account Setup (15 min)
Trainer action:

Open Student Finance.
Select student.
Assign created fee structure.
Save.
Trainee repeats for second student.

Checkpoint:

Student appears in Student Fee Accounts table.
Step 4 – Record Normal Payment (20 min)
Trainer action:

Record a payment without priority allocation.
Save and print receipt.
Explain:

receipt number uniqueness,
auto-allocation behavior,
paid/balance updates.
Checkpoint:

payment appears in Payment Receipts,
balances update.
Step 5 – Record Priority Vote-Head Payment (30 min)
Trainer action:

Tick “Use vote-head priority allocation”.
Enter split (example: Lunch 1500, RMI 500).
Ensure Difference = 0.00.
Save and print.
Trainee practical:

enter a new split with different vote heads.
Checkpoint:

View Vote Heads shows updated paid/balance per vote head,
printed receipt shows allocation details.
Step 6 – Voucher Workflow (20 min)
Trainer action:

Go to Finance.
Create payment voucher.
Search by voucher no.
Print voucher.
Explain signatures:

Principal
Accountant
Payee
Checkpoint:

voucher appears and is printable.
Step 7 – Error Recovery Drills (20 min)
Simulate common issues:

Allocation total mismatch

fix by balancing split total to payment amount.
Missing receipt in filtered list

clear filters / refresh.
Empty vote-head breakdown

verify fee structure assignment and refresh.
Checkpoint: Trainee resolves each issue.

Step 8 – End-of-Day Closing Drill (15 min)
Trainee performs:

total receipts check,
finance transaction check,
print verification,
document filing order.
C. Trainer Assessment Checklist
Mark ✅ when trainee can:

 Login and navigate all finance menus
 Create/edit fee structure
 Assign fee structure to student account
 Record normal payment correctly
 Record priority vote-head payment correctly
 Validate breakdown and balances
 Print receipt and statement
 Create/search/print voucher
 Perform end-of-day reconciliation
D. Certification Note
If trainee passes all checklist items:

“Certified to operate accountant role under supervision.”
